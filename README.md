# Adhyatma Bandha — Smart Health & Safety Wristband

Adhyatma Bandha is a **low-cost smart health and safety wristband** designed to monitor an individual's health condition in crowded environments and provide **early pre-alerts when abnormal conditions are detected**.

The project was developed and submitted for the **Aakruti International Competition organized by Dassault Systèmes, Pune**.

## Project Overview

Adhyatma Bandha is designed as an affordable wearable solution for **real-time health monitoring**. The wristband uses multiple indicators, including a **quad-LED status system and an audible buzzer**, to communicate the user's health status and generate alerts when required.

The primary objective is to make basic health and safety monitoring more accessible through a **low-cost wearable prototype**, with an estimated target cost of **below ₹400**, making the concept suitable for wider adoption.

## Key Features

* **Health monitoring:** Designed to monitor the individual's health condition.
* **Early pre-alert system:** Provides an alert when an abnormal health condition is detected.
* **Quad-LED status indication:** Uses four LED indicators to communicate different health/status conditions.
* **Audible alerts:** Includes a buzzer-based notification mechanism.
* **Low-cost design:** Designed with an estimated target cost of **below ₹400**.
* **Wearable form factor:** Designed as a wristband for convenient use in crowded environments.

## Project Objective

The goal of Adhyatma Bandha is to combine **wearable technology, health monitoring, and an affordable design** into a compact solution that can help provide timely awareness of potential health-related conditions.
